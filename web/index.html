P2P OTC · Native Zcash ↔ Ethereum

Swap shielded ZEC for ETH.
No bridge. No wrapped ZEC.

Alice and Bob agree a price. Before anything is funded, Bob holds a pre-signed ZEC claim and Alice holds a pre-signed, time-locked refund. When Alice takes the ETH, the secret she reveals completes Bob’s shielded ZEC payment. The coordinator never needs the secret.

  • Stays shieldedPrincipal and recipients stay in Ironwood. Ethereum never sees a Zcash address or note.
  • Exits before fundingBoth owners verify their claim or refund, then sign the full terms.
  • Recovers after a crashDurable journals resume the exit without a new signature from the peer.

      How it works

      Four steps, one secret

      FROST DKG and nonces come from the Zcash Foundation libraries. The adaptor extension and its Solidity check are this project’s own and are unaudited.

      1. 01

        Lock a point

        Alice picks a fresh secret scalar t and shares only T = t·G on the Pallas curve.

      2. 02

        Sign the exits first

        A fresh 2-of-2 FROST key controls the ZEC. Bob gets an adaptor pre-signature ŝ for claim C. Alice gets refund R, fully signed and time-locked.

      3. 03

        Claim ETH by revealing

        After the ZEC is funded and Bob locks ETH, Alice calls claim(t). The escrow checks t·G = T and credits her fixed address.

      4. 04

        Complete natively

        Bob reads t and computes s = ŝ + t, a valid RedPallas spend signature. His shielded claim needs nothing more from Alice.

      Not claimed

      • One success-path swap ran on the public testnets; refunds and crash recovery were shown on isolated local chains. No mainnet swap.
      • Both owners run on one machine. Independent hosts are not demonstrated.
      • The custom adaptor signature has no external audit or formal proof.
      • ETH amounts and addresses are public, as is the revealed witness. A refund (only a refund) adds one transparent coin on Zcash.
      • Safety needs monitoring within the timing window; long outages or deep reorgs can break it.
